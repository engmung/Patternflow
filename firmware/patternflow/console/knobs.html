Direction and click size

PositionDirectionEdges per click

Turn a knob clockwise. Its position should go up by one per click. Goes down: set that knob to Reversed. Moves two per click, or needs two clicks to move one: change Edges per click until one click is one step (most encoders are 4; the edge counter under the position shows how many one click really makes). These are properties of the encoder soldered in, stored on the device — nothing to rebuild, and a saved change does not move the knob.
