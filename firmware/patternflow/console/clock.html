Overlay digits are Inter Bold with a soft shadow; the clip styles use Bebas Neue, tall enough that hours over minutes fill an upright panel. Everything is blended into the frame on its way to the panel, so the pattern shows through the edges. Off while the panel's own menus are up.

Time zone

A zone carries its summer-time rule, so the clock moves itself in spring and autumn. The Weather page's UTC offset is the older setting and cannot; when the two disagree, this one wins.
