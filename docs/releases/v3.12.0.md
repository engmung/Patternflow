## [3.12.0] - 2026-10-27

### Firmware

- **A board with Macronix flash mounts its pattern storage.** Core 2.0.17's flash driver read Macronix parts (JEDEC `c2xxxx`) wrong in QIO and DIO, so a Format did not stick and the volume never mounted ([#424](https://github.com/engmung/Patternflow/issues/424)). On a `c2` chip the driver now reads in QOUT, the address on one line and the data still on four, and every other chip is left as it was ([#519](https://github.com/engmung/Patternflow/pull/519) by [@didusee](https://github.com/didusee)). A Macronix board that ran earlier firmware may still hold files written from misread data, and one Format clears them.
- **The MIDI page counts 24 detents a turn, as the encoder has.** The Sensitivity readout worked out its per-turn figure from 20, so 1 : 1 read 20/turn and 6.4 turns end to end, where a turn of the PEC11R-4220F-S0024 sends 24 steps and the sweep takes 5.3. What the panel sends did not change.
- **The vendored HUB75 driver names the upstream commit it came from.** `firmware/patternflow/src/hub75/UPSTREAM.md` gives ESP32-HUB75-MatrixPanel-DMA at tag 3.0.13 (`a622186`), copied on 31 July 2026, and `patternflow.patch` beside it is every change since: `diff -ruN` of upstream's `src/` against the folder, which applied to a clean checkout of the tag gives the folder back. `VENDORED.md` and the comments in `core_display.h` and `core_canvas.h` point at the patch instead of counting the additions. The firmware is unchanged.
- **The vendored HUB75 driver takes upstream's fix for its config constructor.** `HUB75_I2S_CFG` now sets `line_decoder` from its argument (upstream `7bf3b6c`), so the field is `TYPE138` instead of whatever was on the stack where `initDisplay()` builds the config. `src/hub75/UPSTREAM.md` lists the commit and its rebuild cherry-picks it, so `patternflow.patch` still holds only Patternflow's changes and comes out byte for byte the same.
- **The firmware reads the values it shares with the site from `src/core_shared.h`.** `config.h` takes the panel geometries and their sizes from it, `net_config.h` writes the lane scale as 24 detents times two turns, and the encoders, the bus and the sketch take the debounce, the long press and the bus scale. All come from `shared/constants.json`. No value changed, and all five images build to the same size.

### Hardware

- **`check:bom` and `check:bom-schematic` hold the BOM to the build guide and the schematic.** Hard rule 2 as two checks. A designator, an MPN or a quantity that `hardware/bom/bom_v3.9.csv` and the parts tables in section 1 of `BUILD_GUIDE.md` do not share turns `check:ci` red, and so does a designator that the BOM and `hardware/pcb/schematic.pdf` do not share. The schematic's designators come out of its text layer through `pdftotext` (poppler); where it is not installed the check skips, and under `PF_DRIFT_STRICT=1` the skip fails. Part names on the board are not compared, since the guide words them its own way. Off the board, where there is no MPN, the rows pair in order and each guide row keeps every word of its BOM row's part.
- **Besoiobiy's printed case is the second enclosure remix** (`hardware/case/remixes/besoiobiy-printed/`). Two long pieces glued side by side, a frame round the panel and a box for the board and the power bank, closed at the back by four screwed-on covers, with the panel bolted through the case into its own M3 sockets. Its README takes the place of the build guide's sections 4 and 6 and sets the order of 7 to 9; check your panel against it first, because the BOM's panel takes M4 screws on another hole pattern. The build guide's printing section and the assembly map point at it.
- **Mykyta Bilous' horizontal desktop case is the third enclosure remix** (`hardware/case/remixes/mbchars-horizontal-desktop-printed/`, [#488](https://github.com/engmung/Patternflow/pull/488) by [@mbchars](https://github.com/mbchars)). The panel lies the long way across on two removable stands, with the four controls on the right. Three front sections print face down, two white frames and an orange control section, and four joint bars on brass inserts hold them together without glue; two rear covers open onto the electronics and carry wall-mount openings. There is no power-bank bay: a separate PDSink 303PDSink01 trigger module in the control section feeds J4, the screw terminal, at 5 V. One Bambu Studio project holds all nine plates, with illustrated assembly instructions and the BOM changes beside it.
