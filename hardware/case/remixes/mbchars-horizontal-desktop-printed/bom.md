# Bill of materials changes

Use these changes with the original [v3.0 BOM](../../../bom/bom_v3.0.csv). The controller PCB components and LED panel remain unchanged.

## Replacement parts

| Original item | Replacement for this enclosure |
|---|---|
| Original printed enclosure | Parts in the [print table](README.md#print-files) |
| Power bank | External USB-C supply with a 5 V output compatible with the trigger |

## Additional hardware

| Part | Quantity | Specification or location |
|---|---:|---|
| PDSink 303PDSink01 USB Trigger | 1 | Separate module; output set to 5 V |
| Brass heat-set insert | 25 | M3 thread; 5 mm outside diameter; 5 mm length; 4.5 mm printed pilot |
| Joint bar screw | 8 | M3 × 8 mm, countersunk head; two per joint bar |
| USB retainer screw | 1 | M3 × 8 mm, countersunk head |
| Rear cover screw | 16 | M3 × 8 mm, countersunk head |
| Output wire pair | 1 pair | Module +5 V and GND to controller J4 |

The 25 inserts comprise 16 cover inserts, eight joint bar inserts, and one USB retainer insert.

The enclosure uses 25 M3 screws in total.

Do not substitute the enclosure screws for the panel screws.
