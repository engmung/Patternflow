The Seoul Design Festival starts tomorrow. For it, I got up at dawn today, rode over in Hyunji's car, and set up. Setup was done in about an hour, so I kept helping other people and looking around, over and over. That was harder than I expected, and I stayed until closing time and came back home. And since I have to get up early tomorrow, I'm going to write a bit of a blog post and then sleep. From tomorrow I'll probably be completely drained from running the booth, so it's better to write it down ahead of time, right? Though that's the same right now too.

I prepared far more than I first planned. I nailed into the wall and hung the Patternflows up for now, but during the exhibition I think I'll take a few down at a time and stack them out front. Hanging them on a wall like picture frames is a first since I made Patternflow. I designed it for that from the start and even made holes for it, so it's funny that I'm only doing it now, but they stay up well. Controlling the 8 of them with Patternflow Ensemble, a program I made recently, should be really fun.

It's an orchestrating program, sort of. Because of the limits of the ESP32 hotspot it's a maximum of 8 for now, but it can conduct several Patternflows! Did I write about it before? I can't remember. It can also take the real positions of the devices into account and spread an effect across them like a stadium wave. Above all, I set it up so MIDI signals can be transformed and connected in a graph coding? what was it called, that kind of way. I made it like Blender's Geometry Nodes. And I made it so scenes can be set up and switched too.

You can think of Ableton Live as part of the set. Of course other programs would work fine too, but that's what I have, so whatever. Anyway, anyhow, I think I'll try a whole lot of experiments over the 4 days. Ask the people looking around how they'd want to try it, think about how that could be made to work, and try it in real time.

A lot of other things happened recently too. With the Christmas landscape design, I got greedy and tried to get the no-bid contract as Patternflow, but a really complicated situation came up, so I gave up. A little scary and headache-inducing… anyway, the fact that I designed it doesn't go away, and they say it's going ahead as it is and will be installed soon. My role ended a while ago, and it's going ahead as first planned. It's a bit of a shame, but I think it was a good call.

Besides that there are a lot of thoughts and feelings and this and that, but maybe because I'm tired I can't really recall them. My memory hasn't been good at all lately to begin with. Maybe because I've been writing my diary half-heartedly. And I've been skipping exercise a lot too. Hmm… anyway, from tomorrow, let's just enjoy SDF and have fun.
