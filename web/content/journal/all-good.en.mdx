At first I was so angry I meant to write a blog post hating on one person, but now I don't feel like it anymore. I'm running a shared booth, and some really weird people came, calling it a broadcast, and filmed the brand I share it with. While doing that, with no interest at all in my side of the booth, they surrounded the only entrance completely and did their interview right beside me, and it felt a bit like being an animal locked in a cage… not even that, more like the plant next to the cage, and I really hated it. I made it obvious a lot along the way, so they clearly noticed, but they ignored it anyway and pushed ahead, and the so-what attitude was disgusting. When it was over and they were leaving, I said to the people I share the booth with, finally they're gone, what's with those people? When I said that, they glared at me like hell. I should've flipped them off right then instead of smiling and giving a thumbs up…

I was so angry I couldn't really explain it, so I went to them directly to ask and to get an apology… they were seriously shameless. Full of lies, a lip-service apology, said looking me straight in both eyes with a so-what attitude, and I wanted to punch them in the jaw. Saying it was partly my fault for not telling them to stop along the way or whatever, confidently spouting bullshit that doesn't even make sense, that kind of… I was the angriest I've been all year. After that I felt I really couldn't let it go, so on my way out early I told the organizers too, and they said thanks for telling them, that was of course wrong, and that the media outlet hadn't been properly vetted in the first place, it was one that had asked the day before. That they'd take proper action, and thanks for telling them quickly before more problems came up.

I kept asking them whether I'd overreacted. They said not at all. The reason I wanted that confirmation was that when my mom and older brother came to the booth, while I was telling them about those people, the anger welled up and I blew up loudly, and the people running the shared booth apologized and people crowded around, so my mom and brother quickly took me outside. Turns out their train time was really tight so they had to go quickly, and sending me off to a corner outside, at the time it felt a bit like I was a problem kid? Like being isolated, and I was really hurt, but they said they sent me over there because it was windy and cold… My mom is an elementary school teacher, she was pretty scary when I was little, and she works out too so her arm strength is no joke, so I misunderstood.

Anyway, I felt really wronged and angry and kept doubting whether I'd done something wrong, and while I was eating after getting home, my brother told me to call him, so I did. The content was obvious, he was obviously going to give advice about social skills and whatnot. He went on saying my social intelligence is low and pointed out again something I of course know better than anyone, so I got angry too. So while we were both angry I explained my situation in detail, that I was surrounded like in a cage so it was hard to even step away in the middle, and that I had no choice but to put up with it. And I also told him the senior I'd invited came by, talked laughing, and tried to brush it off lightly. Besides that, when I told him about recent times when something unfair happened and I handled it well without getting angry, he said he was glad that what he'd worried about seemed to be getting solved. That he'd worried a lot about that weakness of mine, and he was glad it seemed to be getting fixed.

But this time the timing was really bad and only the ending was a shame, he said, but I'm still young and I can learn as I go. I'd held it in well and introduced things with a forced smile, then my family came and while I was talking the anger welled up, and the people from the shared booth were there and visitors were there too. Maybe if I'd seen them off outside instead, that wouldn't have happened. Anyway, it still ended pretty well. On the phone, when he got angry at me and said I was wrong and something about social intelligence, I felt so wronged that I cried a whole lot too. So now I'm feeling quite a bit better. If it weren't the weekend I'd have gone to the gym and worked out like I was going to die. I left early to do that, but of all days it's the weekend.

Tomorrow might be a bit awkward. Today too, after that happened, it was so awkward, and I could see they were uncomfortable, and I feel like I should say sorry and apologize but I can't… I couldn't even say goodbye, so I just left like I was running away. For now my brother says it'll be fine.

Still, today was really good too. In the morning there seemed to be fewer people so I was disappointed, but after eating with my family it got really crowded. Oh, but we went to Outback for lunch and it was really expensive. I thought it was an all-you-can-eat buffet like Ashley, the Korean buffet chain? It was ridiculously expensive. Honestly the taste wasn't great either… Anyway, I introduced Patternflow to a lot of all kinds of people. Among them there were artists, engineers, someone in a high position at some public institution and so on. Also someone I talked with a lot at the Seoul Design Week network party a while ago came, which was really nice, and above all I met the CEO who 3D prints the Patternflow enclosures! He just came by too, and said it was really amazing to see the products he'd printed before. I also introduced it to people from abroad several times. My English isn't fluent, but it seemed better than yesterday.

Anyway, if I hadn't talked on the phone with my brother and just let the day go by like that, this post would've been 100% full of hate and I'd have really not wanted to go tomorrow either, so I'm glad. Tomorrow I'm going to introduce it excitedly again and enjoy it.
