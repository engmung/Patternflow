I didn't expect that much, but I think applying was a really good call! Yesterday and today I introduced Patternflow to so many people, swapped business cards with people who liked it, and there's this feeling that lots of things are about to happen. Of course only a few of them will actually come true, but let's not throw the feeling away for no reason. Hyojung Seo, a media artist I've liked a lot for a long time, filmed Patternflow and even put it up on their story, so I DMed them too! It felt kind of like talking to a celebrity. I also made chances to get closer with more of the other artists. Anyway, it's really good.

Something out of the ordinary: yesterday a visitor from abroad paid me in dollars and I gave them a grey Patternflow. It was my first time ever touching dollar bills, so they felt a bit like the money from Blue Marble, Korea's Monopoly. If they'd just said they wanted to buy one, I'd have told them to build it themselves as usual, but they said they want to make a big one and wanted to buy this as a sample, and I got swept along. Hmm, they said they'd like to try it at A1 size… is that possible? Honestly I don't know. On top of that, if it's overseas, shipping will be another problem later. Part of me kind of hopes they don't commission it.

Similarly, I kind of wish the funding would just fall apart too. The payout date keeps getting pushed back. They said it'd be done within a week, and what… I'm getting annoyed, and part of me just doesn't want to do it. In a lot of ways the funding feels like a constraint, so I'd rather blame it on them and have it become something that never happened. Still, well, I have to do it. They say the packaging boxes have arrived at the center too. I'll probably go see them on Monday, after SDF is all over.

At SDF I couldn't do a live performance as originally planned. Or is it closer to that I didn't? No, when people come I get distracted and I have to introduce things too, so it's hard. But there was also a lot of time I just sat there spacing out, so what if I'd done it then? Should I do it tomorrow. I probably won't. Still, using the Patternflow Ensemble program, Ableton and the MiniLab 3, I've gotten fairly used to switching scenes and hooking up parameters. That alone is enough, really. Let's work on the performance bit by bit after it's over, practising music too.

All kinds of people come. The reactions are all different too. Still, it's fun too. It's only been yesterday and today, but somehow I think I've found a bit of a way to tell people apart. A way to tell the people who are really interested from the people who leave right away even if I explain? Something like that. I'm tired, so I'll go to sleep soon and get up early again tomorrow and go. Tomorrow will be the peak. Mm-hm. Let's go.
