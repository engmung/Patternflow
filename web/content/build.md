subtitle: Around US$100–200 and an hour of hands-on work.

Every case here holds the same v3 board, soldered, wired and flashed the same way; what changes is the body around it. The official case is printed, and it is the one the build guide walks through. The three community remixes have each been built by their authors: another printed case, one laser-cut from acrylic or MDF sheet, and a printed one that holds the panel the long way across, on stands for a desk. Pick one and the 3D view shows the device in it.
